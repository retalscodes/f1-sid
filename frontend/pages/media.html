Media Room

🎬 F1 For Every Mood

Whether you've watched every race since 1950 or just discovered F1 through Netflix, here's the definitive list of what to watch, read, and listen to. Sid-approved (and Sid is very picky).

📺 Series
🏎️ Series 🦥 Sid's Pick
Drive to Survive
Netflix · 2019–present · 6 seasons
The show that turned F1 from a niche sport into a global cultural moment. Behind-the-scenes access, genuine drama, and enough driver feuds to fuel a year of arguments. Some liberties with the narrative — veteran fans will notice — but for accessibility? Unbeatable.
NetflixBeginner-friendlyDrama
★★★★★
📊 Series
F1: Beyond the Grid
Formula 1 YouTube · 2023–2024
Not a show but a YouTube series — short-form in-depth dives into individual race weekends, technical rule changes, and team profiles. Excellent for bridging the gap between watching races and actually understanding them. The tyre degradation episodes alone are worth it.
YouTubeTechnicalFree
★★★★☆
🔧 Series
The Grand Prix
Netflix · 2024
Netflix's follow-up series focusing specifically on the technical side of F1 — factory visits, wind tunnel sessions, and engineering decisions. More granular than Drive to Survive; recommended for anyone who wants to understand why teams are fast or slow, not just who's feuding.
NetflixEngineering focus
★★★★☆
🎞️ Films
🏆 Film 🦥 Sid's Pick
Rush
2013 · Ron Howard · Chris Hemsworth, Daniel Brühl
The 1976 championship battle between James Hunt and Niki Lauda — two polar opposites who pushed each other to the edge. Arguably the best motorsport film ever made. Lauda's return at Monza after his Nürburgring accident is one of the most remarkable stories in all of sport, and Howard does it full justice.
HistoricalDrama1976 season
★★★★★
🎬 Film
F1 (2025)
2025 · Joseph Kosinski · Brad Pitt, Damson Idris
The first major Hollywood production filmed at actual Grand Prix events with full FIA cooperation. A veteran driver returns to F1 to mentor a young teammate. Shot during real 2023/2024 race weekends, so the crowd, atmosphere, and paddock are genuine. More spectacle than substance, but visually stunning.
2025HollywoodFilmed at real GPs
★★★★☆
🇩🇪 Film
Grand Prix
1966 · John Frankenheimer · James Garner
The original F1 blockbuster — filmed at real 1966 circuits and races. The racing footage is incredible for the era. Technically dated now but historically essential; watch it and appreciate how absurdly dangerous the sport was before safety became a priority.
Classic1966 seasonHistoric
★★★★☆
🇫🇷 Film
Le Mans '66
2019 · James Mangold · Matt Damon, Christian Bale
Not strictly F1 — it's about the 1966 24 Hours of Le Mans and Ford vs Ferrari. But the spirit is identical: the limits of engineering, the tension between drivers and management, and what it means to go fast. Bale as Ken Miles is one of cinema's best racing portrayals.
Le MansEngineering story
★★★★★
🎙️ Documentaries
🇧🇷 Documentary 🦥 Sid's Pick
Senna
2010 · Asif Kapadia · Ayrton Senna
Widely considered the greatest sports documentary ever made. Built entirely from archive footage — no talking heads — it puts you inside Senna's world. His genius, his spirituality, his rivalry with Prost, his death at Imola. Devastating and beautiful. Watch it even if you know nothing about F1.
EssentialAyrton SennaArchive footage
★★★★★
🇩🇪 Documentary
Schumacher
Netflix · 2021 · Michael Schumacher
An intimate look at the seven-time world champion — interviews with family, teammates, and rivals. Made with the support of his family and largely shaped around love. Respectful of his post-2013 condition without exploiting it. A portrait of the most dominant driver the sport has ever seen.
NetflixMichael Schumacher
★★★★☆
🏁 Documentary
1: Life on the Limit
2013 · Paul Crowder
Covers the entire history of Formula 1 from a safety perspective — from the deadly 1950s and 60s through the Lauda accident to modern crash survival. Narrated with archival footage and interviews with drivers across eras. Essential context for why modern F1 safety feels almost miraculous.
HistorySafetyFull archive
★★★★☆
🔵 Documentary
McLaren
2017 · Roger Donaldson · Bruce McLaren
The story of Bruce McLaren — New Zealand engineer and driver who built one of the most successful teams in motorsport history, and died testing his own car at 32. A story about obsession, talent, and the cost of chasing perfection. Heartbreaking in the best way.
Bruce McLarenTeam history
★★★★☆
📚 Books
📖 Book 🦥 Sid's Pick
How to Build a Car
2017 · Adrian Newey
Adrian Newey — the greatest F1 designer in history — walks through his entire career car by car. Part autobiography, part engineering textbook. His descriptions of how he thinks about airflow and downforce are genuinely illuminating. Essential for anyone interested in the technical side of the sport.
EngineeringAdrian NeweyAutobiography
★★★★★
📗 Book
No Filters: My Life in F1
2023 · Christian Horner
Red Bull's team principal details the journey from mid-field team to dynasty — Vettel's four championships, the Hamilton rivalry, Verstappen era. Candid about the internal battles and boardroom politics. Take it with the self-serving bias of any team principal memoir, but the behind-the-scenes detail is excellent.
Team principalRed Bull
★★★★☆
📕 Book
Total Competition
2016 · Ross Brawn & Adam Parr
Ross Brawn — the strategist behind Schumacher's seven championships and the 2009 Brawn GP fairytale — lays out his complete philosophy on building a winning team. Part strategy guide, part F1 history, part management theory. One of the most intelligent books written about the sport.
StrategyRoss BrawnManagement
★★★★★
📘 Book
The Mechanic
2021 · Marc 'Elvis' Priestly
A former McLaren mechanic writes about life inside the factory and paddock — the pitstop choreography, the team culture, the pressure of sub-2-second tire changes. Accessible for non-technical readers. Brings the human side of the thousands of people who never appear on camera but make the cars go fast.
Behind the scenesMcLarenPitstop crew
★★★★☆
🎧 Podcasts
🎙️ Podcast 🦥 Sid's Pick
Beyond the Grid
Formula 1 · Weekly · Tom Clarkson
The official F1 podcast hosted by veteran journalist Tom Clarkson. Long-form interviews with drivers, team principals, engineers, and legends of the sport. Access is unmatched — guests speak more candidly here than almost anywhere else. The Lando Norris episode is a standout.
Official F1InterviewsWeekly
★★★★★
📻 Podcast
The Race F1 Podcast
The Race · Weekly · Various
Sharp analysis from experienced journalists. Less access than Beyond the Grid but often more honest about what's actually happening — politics, technical controversies, steward decisions. Good for race weekend breakdowns if you want more depth than the TV broadcast provides.
AnalysisTechnical
★★★★☆
🎤 Podcast
F1 Nation
Formula 1 · Race weekend · Tom Clarkson & Damon Hill
A more casual, fan-oriented pod featuring a world champion (Hill) discussing the race weekend. Great tone for casual listening — less analytical, more conversational. Damon's insights about the driver's perspective are always worth hearing.
Official F1CasualDamon Hill
★★★★☆
